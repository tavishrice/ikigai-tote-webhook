Floor Time — the effectiveness auditor: hours & output, by day

The headline Hours is the raw window from a person’s first scan to their last (First in → Last out) — the default measure of time on the floor, and the one we trust most today. Active strips 45-min+ breaks out of that window; it’s a stricter, still-maturing calculation, so it sits beside Hours rather than replacing it. The gap between them is break / off-scanner time — log the real off-scanner work under Proj h to keep it honest. Util = active ÷ span; Items/hr = units per floor-hour. All floor work (pick + pack + engrave + restock), Eastern time. Replenishment is credited fairly: because boxes are worked first and scanned in a burst afterward, a long gap right before a replenish scan counts as box work (not a break) — capped at a reasonable per-box amount, so a 2-hour gap before one box can never read as 2 hours of replenishing.
Off-scanner work (returns, cleanup, meetings, training) is logged in the Log time tab — it feeds the Proj h column and the coverage bars above.

Log time — account for real work that doesn’t hit a scanner

Returns, floor resets, receiving, cleanup, meetings, training — the work that never scans. Logging it here closes each person’s unexplained-time gap (see the coverage bars on Floor Time) and feeds their Proj h. Pick a person and a date and their scan schedule appears — tap any gap to fill it in.

Speed leaderboards — units per active hour, fastest first

Only people with enough data are ranked; everyone else is listed as “insufficient” with the reason. Uses the date range above.

Picking speed — MagNano vs Normal — MagNano cases pick much faster

MagNano cases snap together as one strip, so picking them racks up items far faster than a normal pick. This splits each picker’s pace (same median method) on MagNano cases vs everything else, so an easy MagNano-heavy day is obvious.

Where the time goes — tracked hours by activity, per person

Hours actively spent on each task (continuous same-task scans, breaks removed). Answers “how much of the week did each person spend picking vs packing vs engraving.” A floor, not a full timesheet.

Who’s best at what — assignment matrix

Each cell = units/active-hr with a percentile bar within that activity (green = fast). Best fit = the activity where the person ranks highest. Grey dot = has some data but not enough to rank; blank = never did it.

Day plan — who’s in, where they go, and will we clear the orders

Set who’s on the floor and their hours (10 = a full day), and a realistic Utilization % (nobody runs at peak pace for 10 hours straight). Auto-assign pours every person-hour into whatever station is the current bottleneck, using each person’s own all-time pace — so engraving only draws the few hours its demand needs and every spare hour (over-capacity pickers, extra engravers) cascades to the bottleneck. It will split a person’s day when that’s optimal (e.g. 2h engrave + 8h pack, shown next to their output). Override any station, flip in/out, or change hours and it recomputes live. Restock / receiving / returns → Other.
Rates are each person’s own typical pace from Speed & Rankings; a “·” means we don’t have enough data to rate them at that station yet. Watch a person ramp on Trends.

Individual trends — is this person getting faster over time?

One person’s pace (items/hr) per activity, using the robust median method (typical time per item, pauses ignored). Switch Weekly (watch a new hire ramp over months) or Daily (see day-by-day improvement across a week). History starts when scan-tracking began.
